# 📊 Customer Churn Prediction & Survival Analysis

## 🧠 Project Overview

Customer attrition (churn) refers to the loss of customers in subscription-based businesses such as telecom, internet, insurance, and pay TV services. Since retaining existing customers is significantly more cost-effective than acquiring new ones, understanding and predicting churn is a critical business task.

This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior, identifying churn patterns, and building a predictive system that estimates the likelihood of customer attrition.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Perform **customer survival analysis** to understand customer lifetime behavior
* Build a **machine learning model** to predict customer churn probability
* Identify key factors influencing customer attrition
* Develop an **interactive application** to:

  * Explain why a customer may churn
  * Estimate customer lifetime value (CLV)
  * Support data-driven retention strategies

---
